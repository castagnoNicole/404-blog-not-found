Hi everyone! Welcome to my blog!
I'm a third year cybersecurity student, soon to be graduate. 
In this blog I record my cyberecurity *adventures*. They can be conferences that I went to, or workshops I partecipated in. 

## Why am I interested in cybersecurity?
I found my passion for computer science during my last year of high school. After years of studying sociology, anthropology and law I was looking for something different so I started learning how to code for hobby. That was my gateway into everything computer related.

What really attracted me to cybersecurity is the mindset it requires. To protect systems, you have to think like a hacker. Anticipating how someone might try to break in, and finding weaknesses before the real attackers do.

I also appreciate that cybersecurity is as much about people as it is about technology. You can't have a strong security without including people. Everybody needs to be onboard.

For me, cybersecurity is the **perfect mix of technical and people skills**. It challenges both my analytical and interpersonal abilities.
